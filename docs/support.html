Karteria Support

Support for Karteria: Practice with Proof.

Contact

Email tel0955850213@yahoo.com.tw and include the app version, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Do not email private evidence unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

Account and community access

Private practice works without community access. Anonymous peer review requires iCloud, Sign in with Apple, and completion of the 18+ age gate. Users aged 13 to 17 can use invite-only trusted review.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases in Karteria settings when an existing Pro purchase is not shown. Apple manages billing, cancellation, and refunds.

Safety and moderation

Use the in-app report and block controls for anonymous evidence. For urgent removal or moderation requests, email support with the submission identifier shown in the app.

Privacy

Read the Karteria Privacy Policy for details about storage, anonymous review, retention, export, and deletion.
