Karteria Privacy Policy

Effective August 3, 2026

Karteria: Practice with Proof ("Karteria", "we", or "the app") helps people practice skills and verify progress through private practice, invite-only reviews, and optional anonymous peer review.

Information Karteria processes

Private practice information can include goals, levels, schedules, practice records, rubric criteria, notes, photos, and videos. This information is stored on your device and, when iCloud is available, in your private CloudKit database.

If you use anonymous peer review, Karteria processes the evidence you choose to submit, your rubric, review decisions, reports, review credits, and an irreversible salted identifier derived from Sign in with Apple. The public review system does not store your raw Sign in with Apple subject or display your name to reviewers.

If you purchase Karteria Pro, Apple and RevenueCat process purchase status and related identifiers so the app can provide your entitlement. Karteria does not receive your full payment-card details.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide practice, synchronization, evidence review, abuse prevention, moderation, purchase restoration, customer support, export, and deletion features. Karteria does not sell personal information, display behavioral advertising, or track you across apps and websites.

Sharing and visibility

Your daily practice and private Journey are not posted publicly. Invite-only trusted reviews use a CloudKit share controlled by the evidence owner. Anonymous review evidence is visible only to matched reviewers and authorized moderators. Reviewers see evidence and rubric criteria, not the owner's name or contact details.

Do not submit medical judgments, nudity, dangerous or illegal activity, sexual content, relationship disputes, or another person's private information to anonymous review.

Retention

Private practice and Journey data remain until you delete them. Raw anonymous evidence and open review seats are scheduled for deletion 30 days after settlement. Limited review, settlement, reward, and moderation records may be retained to preserve review integrity, prevent abuse, and resolve reports.

Your controls

The app provides controls to export or delete private data, remove remaining anonymous evidence, revoke trusted-review shares, report content, and block future anonymous evidence from the same opaque account identifier. Deleting the app does not automatically delete data already synchronized to iCloud; use the in-app deletion control first.

Children and teenagers

Karteria is intended for people aged 13 or older. Users aged 13 to 17 can use private practice and invite-only trusted review but cannot enter the anonymous public review queue. Anonymous peer review is limited to users aged 18 or older.

Service providers

Karteria uses Apple services, including CloudKit, Sign in with Apple, and StoreKit. RevenueCat is used to manage subscription entitlements. A Cloudflare Worker mediates anonymous review and protects server credentials. Their processing is governed by their respective privacy terms.

Security and limitations

Karteria minimizes public identifiers, keeps server credentials out of the app, and provides media checks before public submission. No transmission or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Avoid including information that is not necessary to demonstrate the rubric.

Changes
